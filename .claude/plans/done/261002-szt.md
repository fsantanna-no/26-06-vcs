    - SZT: Join, seria comecar a participar do FC com um primeiro
      post? Leave, o que seria isso? Simplesmente parar de postar e
      nunca mais se conectar? Descadastrar seu ID? Onde? Connect?

      momento que estamos sincronizando? Ou qualquer post ou
      like/dislike seriam trigger de sincronizacao? Para ter um
      reconnect, precisa ter um connect e um disconnect
    - R [ln 351-360]. A \code{sync} is the only moment two peers
      connect: a short exchange of missing actions (a Git fetch
      and push), after which both sides become offline again.
      There are no servers, brokers, discovery, broadcasts, or any
      underlying network architecture in \FC: only explicit
      point-to-point ephemeral connections. In peer-to-peer terms,
      ``joining'' is cloning, ``leaving'' is simply not syncing,
      and ``reconnecting'' is syncing again. Whom to sync with, and
      how often, is left to users and applications, so replicas
      may legitimately diverge for a long time (S 3.8).
    - R [ln 1000]. peers join, leave, and reconnect at will (S 2).
