# Pass 7: design-level issues (2026-09-21)

## Severe

- [ ] 7.1 the per-identity cap turns reputation weight into identity
      count: at 50K (4.b) every long-lived member weighs the same, so
      fork choice reduces to counting distinct members in a branch;
      a 1000-post member equals a 50-post one; with 3.3's seeding
      incentive, holding many capped identities dominates holding one
      valuable one, and the long-run Sybil defense rests only on each
      identity producing tolerated content
      - the paper presents 4.b only as an incentive to spend
- [ ] 7.2 selective delivery forces a hard fork on a chosen peer,
      cheaply and repeatably: send a branch to victim B only; it
      loses, is appended, settles after 7 days; any later honest
      branch forks before it, so hardfork() refuses and B is split
      until a human runs `discard`
      - profitable: rule 2 refunds the cost in 12h and 1.b pays 1K
        after 24h
      - general form: gossip delivery is asymmetric by nature and the
        settling rule makes any tail difference permanent after the
        window; 3.8.3 assumes convergence within it, but an adversary
        decides whether that holds

## Limitations to state

- [ ] 7.3 one failed action voids every later action in its branch,
      by every member (causality requires it), but the amplification
      appears only as "legitimate actions must be reposted"; worth a
      sentence and a measurement in Section 4
- [ ] 7.4 during idleness the tail never settles: position timestamps
      advance only with accepted actions, so whatever was inside the
      window when activity stopped stays re-orderable indefinitely
