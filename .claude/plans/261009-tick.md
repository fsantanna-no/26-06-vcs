# Paper update: chain clock (tick) replaces per-post timers

# 0. Context

- source: /x/x/freechains/vcs commit e582932 (26/10/08), plan
  `.claude/plans/261008-tick.md`, `doc/reps.md` ln 45-56, 108, 138
- revised 26/10/09 to a single tick (ported to `261007-ia-optim`)
- rule, in short:
    - one clock per chain; tick = `24h * max(0, 1 - 2M/T)`,
      M = reps of members acting since the tick started,
      T = total reps in the chain
    - at each close: refund the 500 of every post since the
      previous tick (rule 2); +1K once per author with a
      non-revoked post (rule 1.b); credit stays on the first post
      even if revoked
    - tick membership by consensus position, not declared time
    - reorg: ticks follow the order; the winner keeps its ticks;
      the loser is squeezed into one later tick, so a farmed branch
      yields one credit per identity at the merge, with no honest
      intervention; its posts are charged at once, most invalidated
- why (sims chat, 5k posts): per-member daily settle left 87% of
  posts queued; reps kept growing after the corpus ended; a 2-day
  conference could admit ~250 of 500 users
- vocabulary: keep "consolidate" for the reward; "settle" stays
  the 7-day window; "tick" (at most 24h), same as vcs; no
  half/full ticks
