# Paper update: chain clock (tick) replaces per-post timers

# 0. Context

- source: /x/x/freechains/vcs commit e582932 (26/10/08), plan
  `.claude/plans/261008-tick.md`, `doc/reps.md` ln 45-56, 108, 138
- rule, in short:
    - one clock per chain; a tick closes after 12h, or sooner in
      proportion to the reps of members acting since it started
      (half of all reps closes it at once)
    - half tick: refunds the 500 of every post since the previous
      half tick (rule 2)
    - full tick (every second tick): +1K to each member with a
      non-revoked post since the previous full tick, one reward
      per member per full tick, whatever the volume (rule 1.b)
    - tick membership by consensus position, not declared time
    - reorg: ticks follow the order; the winner keeps its ticks;
      the loser is flattened, closes no tick (reps minority), so
      a farmed branch yields one credit per member at the merge,
      with no honest intervention
- why (sims chat, 5k posts): per-member daily settle left 87% of
  posts queued; reps kept growing after the corpus ended; a 2-day
  conference could admit ~250 of 500 users
- vocabulary: keep "consolidate" for the reward; "settle" stays
  the 7-day window; new term "tick"
